## The relationship with the MIT license

The MIT license grants broad freedoms to use, copy, modify, and
redistribute the source code. It does not grant rights to the name
"Seekiee," its visual identity, or any associated brand assets. Those are
reserved by Seekiee.

If you fork this project, please **pick your own product name** and your
own visual identity rather than reusing "Seekiee." You are welcome to
describe lineage ("based on Seekiee") with attribution.

## What you can do without asking

- **Fork and modify the code** — per MIT, no permission needed.
- **Describe compatibility and lineage** — "based on Seekiee," "fork of
  Seekiee" with attribution.
- **Name a fork distinctly** — pick your own product name.
- **Educational, journalistic, and personal use** — blog posts,
  tutorials, videos, talks, papers, research.

## What requires written permission

- **Product, service, or company names** that include "Seekiee" or close
  variants.
- **Endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation claims** ("Powered by
  Seekiee," "Official Seekiee," etc.).
- **Logos, wordmarks, color schemes, and visual identity assets** in any
  commercial context.

## How to request permission

Email **hello@darkiee.com** with subject **"Brand request — [your use case]"**.
